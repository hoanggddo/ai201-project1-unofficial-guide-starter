Course: CS211  
Department: Computer Science
University: George Mason University
Source: Rate My Professors
Document Type: Student Reviews

Review:
Grade: A  
Rating: 5/5  
Difficulty: 2/5  
Tags: Caring, Lecture Heavy, Accessible Outside Class  

Student Feedback:

Han's CS211 lectures are somewhat monotonous but concise and focused on material needed for the midterm and final exams. Compared to other computer science courses, students describe the exams as straightforward. Programming assignments are generally manageable except for the final assignment. The reading workload is heavy at the beginning of the semester but becomes lighter later.

Review:
Grade: A-  
Rating: 3/5  
Difficulty: 3/5   
Tags: Get Ready to Read, Lots of Homework, Lecture Heavy  

Student Feedback:

Students describe Professor Han's teaching style as dry and monotonous. The course includes a significant amount of reading during the first few weeks. Some students report changing deadlines, unclear programming assignment grading criteria, lengthy lecture slides, and complex lab exercises. However, exams are generally viewed as easier than the assignments.

Student Feedback:

Students generally describe Professor Han as pleasant but note that lectures can be difficult to follow due to a monotone speaking style.
